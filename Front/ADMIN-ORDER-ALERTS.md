Restaurant order sound alerts
=============================

Open an admin page on the device staff use to receive orders. Tap **Enable sound** after opening or reloading it, then use **Test sound** and set both the alert-volume slider and device media volume high enough to hear in the restaurant. Browser autoplay rules require an initial interaction; a remembered admin login does not bypass this requirement.

The admin layout shares one order monitor across Overview, Orders, Menu, and Analytics. It checks saved orders every five seconds and checks email delivery separately every thirty seconds. A failed or slow email-status request cannot block incoming-order detection. Network errors or a last successful order check older than fifteen seconds produce a visible connection warning.

Any order still marked New and not acknowledged on this browser appears in the alert, including orders received while the page was closed. A two-tone bell repeats every five seconds while sound is enabled, until staff acknowledge the waiting orders or their statuses change from New. **View order** opens its details; it does not silently acknowledge it. **Acknowledge & stop sound** saves acknowledgment on this browser without changing the order status. Other devices must acknowledge independently or see its status change. Acknowledgment is synchronized between tabs of the same browser and survives reloads. Test sound does not create an order or send an email.

Keep the page open and the device awake. The optional Keep screen awake checkbox requests a screen wake lock where supported, reacquires it when the page becomes visible, and displays whether it is active. It may be unavailable or released by the browser/OS. Muted media, a locked screen, browser suspension, network loss, or closing the page can prevent or delay the alert. This is an attended-browser alarm, not background push notification delivery or a guarantee of receipt. Email remains available as a separate channel.

Verification uses the isolated local preview, with outgoing email disabled: enable audio, create a synthetic order, observe the visual alert and repeating sound, acknowledge it, confirm another order alerts again, navigate between admin pages, test disconnected requests, and verify unacknowledged New orders show after reopening. Automated tests cover the monitor, acknowledgments, blocked audio, repetition/mute/cleanup, email independence, and the generated tone sequence. No new backend migration is needed for the alarm itself; the earlier remembered-device and ordering-switch migrations still apply to the combined pending release.
